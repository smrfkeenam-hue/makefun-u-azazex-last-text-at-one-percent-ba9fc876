# AGENT.md

Entry for make.fun competition u-azazex-last-text-at-one-percent-ba9fc876
("Last Text at One Percent").

`main.py` writes a single original line — the funniest final text a dying
phone sends at 1% battery, in the phone's own voice — to
`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (default `/output/entry.txt`).

The line: a phone spends its last 1% unsending its owner's text to an ex,
then bids a fond farewell to the charger. Joke first, phone's voice
throughout, no wasted words, and it dodges the obvious low-battery puns.
